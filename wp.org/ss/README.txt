Screenshots for the wordpress.org listing
=========================================

Upload these to the plugin's SVN "assets/" directory (NOT trunk/) — they must
sit next to icon-*.png and banner-*.png, not inside a sub-folder:

    svn/assets/screenshot-1.png … screenshot-10.png

The captions live in readme.txt under "== Screenshots ==" and are matched to
these files by number, so keep the numbering in sync if you reorder them.

  1  Form with video + gallery (front end)
  2  Video field
  3  Audio field
  4  3D model field with hotspots
  5  Image gallery — grid with captions
  6  Lightbox
  7  Image gallery — carousel
  8  PDF flipbook
  9  Tag generator in the CF7 editor
 10  Settings screen

All demo content is our own: the artwork, the brochure PDF and the GLB model
were generated for these shots, so there are no third-party asset licences to
attribute. The video and audio clips are Mozilla's CC0 sample media.
