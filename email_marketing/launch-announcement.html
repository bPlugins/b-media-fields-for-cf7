You are getting this because you use one of our plugins. Today we put a new one on wordpress.org, and if you also build forms with Contact Form 7 it may save you some work.

It is called Media Fields for Contact Form 7, and it puts media inside the form itself: video, audio, 3D models, image galleries and page turning PDFs. It is free, and it is at version 1.1.0 as of today.

Watch the 2 minute introduction to see all five fields in a real form.

The form is usually where someone decides whether to get in touch. Showing them the work, the product or the brochure at that exact moment, without sending them to another page, is the whole idea.

Five fields you can drop in
[video] Self hosted files, YouTube or Vimeo, with a proper accessible player
[audio] Briefs, episodes and voice notes, right above the questions
[3d_models] glTF and GLB with hotspots, and AR on supported phones
[gallery] Grid, masonry, justified rows or carousel, with a built in lightbox
[pdf_flipbook] Catalogues and manuals that turn like paper, with zoom and fullscreen
 

Every field has its own button in the Contact Form 7 editor, so there is no syntax to remember.

Try it, free on wordpress.org

Or search for "Media Fields for Contact Form 7" under Plugins, then Add New, inside your site.

It is new, so we would genuinely like to know how it holds up on your sites. A signature field and an image choice field are next on our list. If you try it, reply and tell us what worked and what did not. We read every reply.
