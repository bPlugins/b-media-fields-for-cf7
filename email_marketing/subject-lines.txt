Subject lines
=============

Keep them under about 45 characters so they are not cut off on phones.
No emoji, on purpose: both of these go to people who already know us, and
plain subjects read as product news rather than promotion.


A. Existing users of this plugin  (1.1.0-announcement.*)
--------------------------------------------------------
  A1  Two new fields in Media Fields for CF7        (44)
  A2  Your forms can now show a gallery and a PDF   (43)
  A3  Image gallery and PDF flipbook are here       (39)
  A4  Media Fields 1.1.0 is out                     (25)

  Preheader:
    Your forms can now show an image gallery and a page turning PDF, on top
    of video, audio and 3D.


B. Subscribers of our other plugins  (launch-announcement.*)
------------------------------------------------------------
  B1  We released a new plugin today                (30)
  B2  New today: media fields for Contact Form 7    (43)
  B3  A new plugin from bPlugins, for CF7 users     (43)
  B4  Put video, audio and 3D inside your CF7 forms (45)

  Preheader:
    Free on wordpress.org. Video, audio, 3D models, galleries and PDFs,
    inside your Contact Form 7 forms.

  B1 is the safest opener for this list: it says what happened without
  assuming the reader uses Contact Form 7. The email itself qualifies the
  reader in the first two lines, so people who do not use CF7 can stop
  reading early instead of feeling mis sold.
