It is called Media Fields for Contact Form 7, and it puts media inside the

Watch the 2 minute introduction:
https://youtu.be/sbw-mq7Yugs

The form is usually where someone decides whether to get in touch. Showing
them the work, the product or the brochure at that exact moment, without
sending them to another page, is the whole idea.


FIVE FIELDS YOU CAN DROP IN

  [video]         Self hosted files, YouTube or Vimeo, with a proper
                  accessible player
  [audio]         Briefs, episodes and voice notes, right above the questions
  [3d_models]     glTF and GLB with hotspots, and AR on supported phones
  [gallery]       Grid, masonry, justified rows or carousel, with a built in
                  lightbox
  [pdf_flipbook]  Catalogues and manuals that turn like paper, with zoom and
                  fullscreen

Every field has its own button in the Contact Form 7 editor, so there is no
syntax to remember.


Try it, free on wordpress.org:
https://wordpress.org/plugins/b-media-fields-for-cf7/

Or search for "Media Fields for Contact Form 7" under Plugins, then Add New,
inside your site.
