Watch the 2 minute introduction:

The form is usually where someone decides whether to get in touch. Showing
them the work, the product or the brochure at that exact moment, without
sending them to another page, is the whole idea.


FIVE FIELDS YOU CAN DROP IN

[video]         Show the tour, then take the booking.
                Estate agents, studios and venues: the film plays inside the
                form, so nobody has to leave the page to watch it.

[audio]         Let them hear it before they ask.
                Podcasts, voice work and coaching: a clip or a spoken brief
                sits directly above the questions.

[3d_models]     Spin the product, then quote it.
                Made to order furniture, signage and fabrication: the customer
                turns the piece and picks a finish in the same form.

[gallery]       Show the work before the quote request.
                Builders, photographers and designers: recent jobs in a grid,
                with a dropdown so they tell you which one they mean.

[pdf_flipbook]  Put the catalogue in the form.
                Wholesalers, manufacturers and schools: browse the brochure and
                request access, with no download first.

Every field has its own button in the Contact Form 7 editor, so there is no
syntax to remember.
